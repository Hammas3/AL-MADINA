Our Story

WELCOME TO AL MADINA BIRYANI HOUSE

Located in the heart of Karachi, Pakistan, Al Madina Biryani House is committed to serving authentic, aromatic, and rich Karachi-style Biryani alongside classic Desi delicacies.

Every serving is a result of selected spices, quality rice, and passion for Pakistani culinary tradition.
